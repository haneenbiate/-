# Task 07 methodology and current scientific status

The completed Task 06 ZIP is verified against its 15 registered payload hashes,
original input identities and selected classifier/tokenizer hashes; both metric
tables are independently recomputed from 62,350 real saved predictions. GPU
provenance reports Tesla T4 and no training/test access. Original Task 06 and Task
05 evidence is preserved. Local weight verification/reload is not claimed.

V1 normalization and selected-tokenizer outputs are replayed for all 62,350 records.
Among 4,948 applicable elongations, 4,864 have identical B1–B4 effective inputs
and 4,945 identical predicted classes; the reviewed 89/94 elongation cohorts all
agree. Narrow outcome allowlists, the run threshold, lexical protection and
tokenization explain most inert behavior, not a universal identical-prediction
claim. All 84 token exceptions and 643 B3/B4 text/input differences are audited.
287/290 differences per tatweel intensity are structural typography-only candidates
outside possible markers, not human-certified semantic equivalence. Numeric
paired B3/B4 differences and all-clean scores are separately reported.

Original annotation records have unverified identity/independence/blinding. No
new genuine independent-human agreement is calculated. Source eligibility is
user-declared; 394 eligible, 9,557 unreviewed provisional and 10 excluded variants
are distinct, and excluded variants receive no robustness metrics.

Frozen v2 uses run length >=3 capped at 2, literal possible-marker preservation,
fixed sentiment/phrase protection and explicit character restrictions. Simple
tatweel, repetition and combined baselines are included. The combined baseline
is the context ablation; NASN2's context guard blocks repetition only, not nearby
tatweel. No labels, predictions, IDs, training frequencies or test information
select edits. All 62,350 v2 records restore exactly with private logs and have
selected-tokenizer audits. The 188 new human-review assignments are blank, with
separate blinded reviewer ZIPs and explicit independent submission/adjudication
workflow. No source approval propagates to new normalized text.

New v2 classifier comparison is BLOCKED locally by absent selected trained weights
and CUDA. Its numerical metrics are unavailable, not inferred from token counts
or copied from old B1/B3. The scientific decision is UNDETERMINED. The prepared
GPU CLI verifies all same-checkpoint bytes and RAW clean baseline predictions,
then reports per-rule and pooled original-ID-cluster paired intervals. The frozen
performance gate compares NASN2 to every simple baseline; failure recommends
abandoning the improvement claim or reframing as a preprocessing audit instead
of post-hoc rules. A passed gate is still provisional until semantic/provenance
requirements are met. No novelty or corrected-significance claim is made.

Full rules, counterexamples, limits, hashes, private artifact paths and exact
commands are in [the reproducible report](../../docs/task07.md) and
[the frozen protocol](../../docs/task07_protocol.md). The generated Task 07 Colab
notebook is pinned and syntax-tested, not executed here. Raw text, reviewer
identities/notes, token IDs and weights remain private; registered numeric light
Task 06 evidence is preserved byte-for-byte. No official test data is evaluated,
modified, or used for tuning, and no model is trained.
